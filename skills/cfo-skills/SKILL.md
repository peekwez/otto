# Couqley French Bistro Brand Guidelines

This skill ensures all materials created for Couqley French Bistro follow the established brand identity, visual standards, and financial reporting formats as defined in the 2017 Brand Profile.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when creating:

- Marketing materials and presentations
- Business documents and reports
- Financial presentations and analyses
- Internal communications
- Brand collateral of any kind

## Brand Identity

### Brand Name

**Couqley French Bistro**

- Always maintain the exact capitalization: "Couqley" (not "COUQLEY" in body text)
- Tagline: "FRENCH BISTRO" (in caps, positioned beneath the main logo)

### Brand Positioning

Couqley is a French bistro concept focused on authentic French cuisine in a warm, approachable bistro setting.

## Visual Guidelines

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**

- **Cream/Beige Background**: RGB(247, 243, 233) or #F7F3E9
  - Use for primary backgrounds and elegant, neutral spaces
  - Creates warmth and sophistication
- **Couqley Red**: RGB(204, 51, 51) or #CC3333

  - Primary brand color for logos, headers, and accents
  - Use for emphasis and brand recognition elements
  - Can be used in gradient or solid form

- **Gold/Tan Accent**: RGB(191, 153, 102) or #BF9966
  - Use for borders, decorative elements, and secondary accents
  - Complements the cream background
  - Adds sophistication and French bistro elegance

**Usage Guidelines:**

- Cream/beige should dominate as the primary background
- Red is used sparingly for impact (logos, key headers, data highlights)
- Gold/tan frames and accents content without overwhelming
- Maintain high contrast for readability

### Typography

**Primary Font:**

- Use a classic serif font or elegant sans-serif for body text
- Headers: Bold, clean sans-serif in Couqley Red or gold
- All caps for "FRENCH BISTRO" tagline
- All caps for major section headers (e.g., "FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS")

**Text Hierarchy:**

- Main headers: Large, bold, Couqley Red
- Section headers: Medium, bold, uppercase
- Body text: Professional, readable serif or sans-serif in dark grey/black
- Financial data: Clean, tabular formatting with clear hierarchy

### Logo Usage

The Couqley logo consists of:

1. **Main wordmark**: "COUQLEY" in red with distinctive styling
2. **Tagline**: "FRENCH BISTRO" in smaller text with decorative flourishes
3. **Border treatment**: Elegant gold/tan border frames with curved corners

**Logo Placement:**

- Top right corner of presentations and documents
- Centered on title slides
- Always include both the wordmark and "FRENCH BISTRO" tagline
- Maintain adequate white space around the logo

### Design Elements

**Borders and Frames:**

- Use elegant, curved-corner frames in gold/tan (RGB(191, 153, 102))
- Double-line borders for formal documents
- Decorative flourishes (subtle curves) at corners
- Consistent border weight throughout materials

**Layout Principles:**

- Clean, spacious layouts with generous white space
- Symmetrical, balanced compositions
- Classical, elegant design aesthetic
- Avoid cluttered or overly modern designs

## Financial Reporting Standards

When creating financial materials for Couqley, follow these established formats:

### Financial Scenario Analysis Format

**Structure:**

1. Use clear scenario labeling: "Base", "Target", "Bear"
2. Present 5-year projections (Year 1 through Year 5 plus Total)
3. Include both absolute values and percentages

**Revenue Projections:**

- Display in clear tabular format
- Show progression across years
- Include total column
- All figures in USD

**EBITDA Reporting:**

- Show both dollar amounts and percentages
- Calculate EBITDA % for each scenario and year
- Maintain consistent percentage formatting (e.g., 28%, 27%, 26%)

**Visual Presentation:**

- Headers in Couqley Red background with white text
- Alternating row colors (light background) for readability
- Bold column headers
- Right-align numerical data
- Use consistent number formatting (commas for thousands)

### Charts and Graphs

**Daily Average Covers Analysis:**

- Use horizontal bar charts for year-over-year comparisons
- Color coding:
  - **Bear scenario**: Couqley Red (RGB(204, 51, 51))
  - **Base scenario**: Tan/beige (RGB(210, 180, 140))
  - **Target scenario**: Light tan/cream (RGB(228, 210, 180))
- Include numerical labels on bars
- Clear year labels (Year 1, Year 2, etc.)
- Legend placement at bottom

**Data Visualization Best Practices:**

- Use brand colors exclusively
- Ensure high contrast and readability
- Include clear axis labels
- Show data values on charts when space permits
- Maintain consistency across all visualizations

## Content Guidelines

### Tone of Voice

- Professional yet approachable
- Sophisticated without being pretentious
- Warm and inviting (reflecting bistro atmosphere)
- Data-driven when presenting financials
- Confident in projections and capabilities

### Writing Style

- Clear, concise business language
- French terminology used sparingly and appropriately
- Financial language should be precise and professional
- Avoid jargon unless industry-standard

## Document Types

### Presentations (PowerPoint/Keynote)

**Title Slide:**

- Centered logo with full branding
- Cream/beige background
- Gold border frame
- Document title in elegant typography
- Subtitle/date if applicable

**Content Slides:**

- Logo in top right corner
- Section headers in Couqley Red on red background
- Body content on cream/beige background
- Gold accent borders when appropriate
- Consistent footer (company name, date, page numbers)

**Financial Slides:**

- Clean tabular layouts
- Red headers for tables
- Consistent number formatting
- Charts using brand color palette
- Clear labels and legends

### Documents (Word/PDF)

**Header:**

- Logo placement (top right or centered)
- Document title
- Date and version if applicable

**Body:**

- Professional formatting
- Consistent margins
- Brand color accents in headers
- Tables and charts following financial reporting standards

**Footer:**

- Page numbers
- Confidentiality statements if needed
- Company name

## Technical Specifications

### File Formats

- Presentations: .pptx (PowerPoint)
- Documents: .docx (Word) or .pdf
- Images: .png (high resolution, 300 dpi minimum)
- Charts: Vector format when possible

### Dimensions

- Presentation slides: 16:9 ratio (standard)
- Documents: Letter size (8.5" x 11") or A4
- Logo minimum size: 1 inch width
- Margins: 1 inch on all sides for documents
